# Alumnia sector-first film — storyboard and source credits

## Direction

A 16:9, two-minute editorial motion-design film: fast but legible cuts, short punch-ins, bold kinetic type and white framed panels inspired by contemporary culture/design explainers. Use Alumnia’s Bricolage Grotesque + Figtree, green `#1C8247`, AI blue `#1D63B8`, paper white and rounded panels. Yellow appears only for moments of progress. The first 10 seconds contrast real street footage with the clean white system; protest audio is kept low and fades after second 3.
